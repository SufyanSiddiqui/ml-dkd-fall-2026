Decoupled Knowledge Distillation — Complete 23-Step Algebraic Derivation

Every single algebraic rule shown one micro-step at a time, with zero skipped steps from raw logits to the final PyTorch loss.

\(C = 100\) & \(t \in \{1..C\}\) Total classes (\(100\)) and the ground-truth target class index \(t\) of the current image.
\(z_i^T,\; z_i^S\) Raw pre-softmax output logits from the Teacher (\(T\)) and Student (\(S\)) for class \(i\).
\(\mathbf{b} = [p_t,\; p_{\setminus t}]\) 2-bucket binary probability vector: target probability vs. sum of all 99 non-target probabilities.
\(\hat{\mathbf{p}} = [\hat{p}_i]_{i \neq t}\) 99-class conditional non-target distribution (renormalized so the 99 wrong classes sum to \(1\)).
Phase I (Steps 1–4): From Raw Logits to Classical Hinton KD
1 Start with Teacher & Student raw logit vectors
Both networks take one image \(\mathbf{x}\) (true class \(t\)) and output \(C\) unbounded real scores.
$$\mathbf{z}^T = [z_1^T, z_2^T, \dots, z_C^T] \in \mathbb{R}^C, \qquad \mathbf{z}^S = [z_1^S, z_2^S, \dots, z_C^S] \in \mathbb{R}^C$$
2 Convert logits into temperature-softened probabilities via Softmax
Dividing logits by temperature \(T > 0\) before exponentiating flattens sharp peaks so small probabilities become visible.
$$p_i^T = \frac{\exp(z_i^T / T)}{\sum_{j=1}^{C} \exp(z_j^T / T)}, \qquad p_i^S = \frac{\exp(z_i^S / T)}{\sum_{j=1}^{C} \exp(z_j^S / T)} \qquad \text{for } i = 1, \dots, C$$
3 Write the Kullback–Leibler (KL) divergence between \(p^T\) and \(p^S\)
KL divergence measures the information lost when the Student distribution \(p^S\) approximates the Teacher distribution \(p^T\).
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = \sum_{i=1}^{C} p_i^T \ln\left(\frac{p_i^T}{p_i^S}\right)$$
4 Define Hinton's Classical KD Loss (and hold \(T^2\) aside while we simplify KL)
Classical KD scales \(\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S)\) by \(T^2\). Let us first expand \(\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S)\) cleanly, then multiply back by \(T^2\) at the end.
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{KD}} = T^2 \, \mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S)$$
Phase II (Steps 5–9): Splitting Target Mass from Non-Target Mass
5 Pull the single target term (\(i = t\)) out of the \(C\)-class summation
A sum over all \(100\) classes is simply the \(1\) true class \(t\) plus the sum over the remaining \(99\) non-target classes (\(i \neq t\)).
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} p_i^T \ln\left(\frac{p_i^T}{p_i^S}\right)$$
6 Define total non-target probability mass \(p_{\setminus t}\)
Since all \(C\) probabilities sum to \(1\), the combined probability of all \(99\) wrong classes equals \(1 - p_t\).
$$p_{\setminus t} \;\triangleq\; \sum_{j \neq t} p_j \;=\; 1 - p_t \qquad \text{(applies identically to } p_{\setminus t}^T \text{ and } p_{\setminus t}^S\text{)}$$
7 Form the 2-element binary distribution \(\mathbf{b}\)
\(\mathbf{b}\) is a valid 2-class probability distribution (\([p_t, \, 1-p_t]\)) answering: "Target class vs. Everything else?"
$$\mathbf{b}^T = [p_t^T,\; p_{\setminus t}^T], \qquad \mathbf{b}^S = [p_t^S,\; p_{\setminus t}^S], \qquad \text{where } p_t + p_{\setminus t} = 1$$
8 Define the conditional non-target probability \(\hat{p}_i\) for every \(i \neq t\)
Divide each non-target probability \(p_i\) by total non-target mass \(p_{\setminus t}\). Notice the full softmax denominators cancel out, and \(\sum_{i \neq t}\hat{p}_i = 1\)!
$$\hat{p}_i \;\triangleq\; \frac{p_i}{p_{\setminus t}} \;=\; \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_{j \neq t} \exp(z_j / T)} \quad (\forall\, i \neq t), \qquad \text{with } \sum_{i \neq t} \hat{p}_i = \frac{\sum_{i \neq t} p_i}{p_{\setminus t}} = 1$$
9 Multiply both sides of Step 8 by \(p_{\setminus t}\) to get the key substitution rule
Every individual non-target probability \(p_i\) is the product of total non-target mass \(p_{\setminus t}\) and conditional share \(\hat{p}_i\).
$$p_i^T = p_{\setminus t}^T \cdot \hat{p}_i^T \qquad \text{and} \qquad p_i^S = p_{\setminus t}^S \cdot \hat{p}_i^S \qquad (\forall\, i \neq t)$$
Phase III (Steps 10–16): Expanding the Non-Target Sum One Rule at a Time
10 Substitute \(p_i^T = p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T\) and \(p_i^S = p_{\setminus t}^S \hat{p}_i^S\) into Step 5
Replace \(p_i^T\) and \(p_i^S\) inside the \(i \neq t\) summation using Step 9.
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} \left(p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T\right) \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S \hat{p}_i^S}\right)$$
11 Separate the fraction inside the logarithm into two multiplied ratios
Use basic fraction multiplication: \(\frac{A \cdot B}{C \cdot D} = \left(\frac{A}{C}\right)\left(\frac{B}{D}\right)\).
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left[\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right)\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right)\right]$$
12 Apply the logarithm product identity \(\ln(X \cdot Y) = \ln X + \ln Y\)
Turn the log of a product into the sum of two logs.
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T \left[ \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right) + \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right) \right]$$
13 Distribute \(p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T\) across the brackets
Multiply \(p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T\) onto both log terms inside the summation.
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} \left[ p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right) \;+\; p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right) \right]$$
14 Split the summation of two terms into two separate summations
Use linearity of summation: \(\sum (u_i + v_i) = \sum u_i + \sum v_i\).
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right) \;+\; \sum_{i \neq t} p_{\setminus t}^T \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right)$$
15 Factor terms that do NOT depend on index \(i\) outside each summation
Neither \(p_{\setminus t}^T\) nor \(\ln(p_{\setminus t}^T / p_{\setminus t}^S)\) depends on \(i\), so pull them in front of the \(\sum_{i \neq t}\) signs!
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) \;+\; p_{\setminus t}^T \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right) \underbrace{\left(\sum_{i \neq t} \hat{p}_i^T\right)}_{=\,1} \;+\; p_{\setminus t}^T \sum_{i \neq t} \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right)$$
16 Apply \(\sum_{i \neq t} \hat{p}_i^T = 1\) (proven in Step 8)
Because the 99 conditional non-target probabilities sum to \(1\), the middle summation simplifies to multiplying by \(1\)!
$$\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S) = \left[ p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) + p_{\setminus t}^T \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right) \right] \;+\; p_{\setminus t}^T \left[ \sum_{i \neq t} \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right) \right]$$
Phase IV (Steps 17–20): Recognizing TCKD, NCKD & the Suppression Flaw
17 Recognize the first bracket as \(\mathrm{TCKD} = \mathrm{KL}(\mathbf{b}^T \parallel \mathbf{b}^S)\)
Look at the two terms in the first bracket: they are the exact 2-class KL divergence between \(\mathbf{b}^T = [p_t^T, p_{\setminus t}^T]\) and \(\mathbf{b}^S = [p_t^S, p_{\setminus t}^S]\)!
$$\mathrm{TCKD} \;\triangleq\; \mathrm{KL}(\mathbf{b}^T \parallel \mathbf{b}^S) \;=\; p_t^T \ln\left(\frac{p_t^T}{p_t^S}\right) + p_{\setminus t}^T \ln\left(\frac{p_{\setminus t}^T}{p_{\setminus t}^S}\right)$$
18 Recognize the second bracket as \(\mathrm{NCKD} = \mathrm{KL}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}^T \parallel \hat{\mathbf{p}}^S)\)
The remaining sum over \(i \neq t\) is the exact 99-class KL divergence between the non-target distributions \(\hat{\mathbf{p}}^T\) and \(\hat{\mathbf{p}}^S\)!
$$\mathrm{NCKD} \;\triangleq\; \mathrm{KL}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}^T \parallel \hat{\mathbf{p}}^S) \;=\; \sum_{i \neq t} \hat{p}_i^T \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}_i^T}{\hat{p}_i^S}\right)$$
19 Substitute \(p_{\setminus t}^T = 1 - p_t^T\) and restore \(T^2\) — Exact Classical KD Identity!
Substitute Steps 17 & 18 into Step 16 (and multiply back by \(T^2\) from Step 4). Zero approximations were made anywhere!
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{KD}} \;=\; T^2 \Big[\; 1 \cdot \mathrm{TCKD} \;+\; (1 - p_t^T) \cdot \mathrm{NCKD} \;\Big]$$
20 Plug in numbers to see WHY Classical KD fails on confident Teacher predictions
When the Teacher clearly recognizes an image (\(p_t^T = 0.99\)), its ranking of visually similar cousin classes (\(\mathrm{NCKD}\)) is super reliable—yet \((1 - p_t^T)\) crushes \(\mathrm{NCKD}\) by \(100\times\)!
$$p_t^T = 0.99 \;\Longrightarrow\; (1 - p_t^T) = 0.01 \quad \text{(99\% of Dark Knowledge destroyed!)}$$
Phase V (Steps 21–23): The DKD Fix, \(T^2\) Gradient Scaling & Total Training Objective
21 Decouple the weights: replace \(1\) with \(\alpha\) and replace \((1 - p_t^T)\) with constant \(\beta\)
Free \(\mathrm{NCKD}\) from Teacher confidence \(p_t^T\)! On CIFAR-100 (\(\texttt{ResNet32x4} \to \texttt{ResNet8x4}\)), \(\alpha = 1.0\) and \(\beta = 8.0\).
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DKD}} \;=\; T^2 \Big(\; \alpha \cdot \mathrm{TCKD} \;+\; \beta \cdot \mathrm{NCKD} \;\Big)$$
22 Why multiply by \(T^2\)? (Chain-rule gradient preservation)
Differentiating \(\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S)\) with respect to student logit \(z_i^S\) pulls out \(1/T\), and for large \(T\), \(p_i^S - p_i^T \propto (z_i^S - z_i^T)/T\) pulls out a second \(1/T\). Multiplying by \(T^2\) cancels \(1/T^2\) so gradients don't vanish at \(T=4, 10, 100\)!
$$\frac{\partial\,\mathrm{KL}(p^T \parallel p^S)}{\partial z_i^S} = \frac{1}{T}(p_i^S - p_i^T) \;\approx\; \frac{1}{C\,T^2}\big((z_i^S - \bar{z}^S) - (z_i^T - \bar{z}^T)\big) \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; T^2 \cdot \frac{\partial\,\mathrm{KL}}{\partial z_i^S} = \mathcal{O}(1)$$
23 Final Student Training Loss (with 20-epoch linear warmup in PyTorch)
Combine standard hard-label Cross-Entropy (\(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CE}} = -\ln p_t^S(T{=}1)\)) with linearly warmed-up \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DKD}}\) so \(\beta = 8.0\) doesn't overwhelm random Epoch-1 weights.
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{total}}(\text{epoch}) \;=\; \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CE}}(\mathbf{z}^S, t) \;+\; \min\!\left(\frac{\text{epoch}}{20},\, 1.0\right) \cdot T^2 \Big(\alpha\,\mathrm{KL}(\mathbf{b}^T \parallel \mathbf{b}^S) + \beta\,\mathrm{KL}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}^T \parallel \hat{\mathbf{p}}^S)\Big)$$
